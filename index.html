OS32

A tiny OS for an ESP32 with a 128x64 OLED. Plug in your ESP32 with a USB data cable, then press Install (Chrome or Edge only).

Wiring

  • OLED (SSD1306): SDA to GPIO21, SCL to GPIO22
  • Joystick: VRx to GPIO34, VRy to GPIO35, button to GPIO32
  • Buzzer to GPIO33 (optional), MPU-6050 on the same SDA/SCL (optional)
